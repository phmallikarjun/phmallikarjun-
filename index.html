About Me

About Me

Hi, I’m Mallikarjun H, a UI/UX Designer based in Bengaluru with a passion for creating clean, modern, and user-friendly digital experiences. I enjoy turning ideas into intuitive interfaces that not only look visually appealing but also solve real user problems.

My work focuses on user research, wireframing, prototyping, and visual design, with strong attention to usability, accessibility, and design systems. I’m skilled in Figma, HTML, CSS, React.js, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, and Premiere Pro.

I’ve worked on real-world projects like Fixigo, where I designed a home service mobile app with a seamless booking experience, and also created its complete brand identity through a logo case study. I love combining creativity with functionality to build meaningful digital products that feel simple and enjoyable to use.

I’m always exploring new design trends, improving my design process, and creating experiences that make technology easier and more enjoyable for people to use.
